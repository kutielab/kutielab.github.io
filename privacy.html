KutieLab Poster – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. About this app

KutieLab Poster (“the App”) is a private, internal tool operated by KutieLab, a small studio that makes custom pet-shaped neckties. It publishes KutieLab’s own videos to KutieLab’s own TikTok account.

2. What we collect

The App does not collect personal information from the public, customers or visitors. The only data it handles is the access token that TikTok gives it after the KutieLab account owner logs in, plus the videos and captions KutieLab chooses to post.

3. How the data is used

The access token is used only to publish KutieLab’s own content to KutieLab’s own TikTok account. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and sharing

The token is stored privately on the account owner’s own computer. We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, other than sending posts to TikTok itself.

5. Retention and deletion

The token is kept only while the App is in use. The account owner can revoke the App’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings, and the stored token is then deleted.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children and does not collect information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
